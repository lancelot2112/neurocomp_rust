generation 2 (the reader survives its own voice being heard). With the three-factor rule all three
get there, and fastest (pm4 by 20 to 32); without it two of three (pm4 at 29 and 137); with rule
tables two of three. None passes pm2 (a quarter of each sound's lines from its voice code): the
echo there is mostly wrong, and nothing has learned the sounds.
